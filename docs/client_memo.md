## What I found

Of the 127 titles on your list, 112 could be analysed; 10 entries were blanks, repeats or ids from another system, and 5 matched no film. For each film I compared box-office revenue with its production budget, as of 20 November 2026.

Franchise films earned more for each dollar spent than standalone films, and this holds whether or not pre-2000 figures are included. The typical franchise film returned 5.6 times its budget against 3.8 for standalones; counting only films from 2000 onward, 5.1 against 3.8. No data-handling choice I made changes this.

**The titles to buy change depending on the years you trust.** On all years, the strongest franchises are Star Wars, The Lord of the Rings and Indiana Jones, and the strongest standalones are five films from 1966 to 1994. From 2000 onward, the period your finance team can stand behind, the list is different, and Star Wars drops from 2.5 to 1.3 times the typical film once its later films are compared only with films of the same period.

Later films in a franchise earn about 70% of what the first film earned; the middle films are no weaker than the latest. The first film carries much of a bundle's value.

## What I recommend

Prioritise franchise bundles. Shortlist, based on films from 2000 onward:

- **Franchises:** The Lord of the Rings, Ice Age, The Avengers. Even their weakest films earned at least 1.2 times the typical film.
- **Standalones:** Spirited Away, Inception, Interstellar, Memento, The Departed. Only the first two beat the typical film; standalones are the weaker buy.
- On pre-2000 figures alone, Star Wars, Indiana Jones and five classic standalones would also qualify.

**Deal note: The Lord of the Rings.** It ranks second over all years and first from 2000 onward. Each of its films returned 2.4 times what a typical film from the same period returned; that is the number that drove this view. Its weakest film, the first, still returned 1.9 times. Pay up to about 1.9 times, per film, what you would pay for a typical title; above that, the weakest film no longer supports the price. Caution: only three of its films were on your list.

**Confidence: moderate.** The direction is firm; the exact titles and margins are less so, because only 8 standalone films from 2000 onward were available to compare.

**What would change this:** your own viewing figures. The two standalones that earned least at the box office, The Shawshank Redemption and Fight Club, are among the most widely watched on the list. If viewing, not box office, drives your value, standalones may be worth more than this shows.
