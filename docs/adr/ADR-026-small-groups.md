# ADR-026: Three films to rank a bundle, five for a group conclusion, and every figure carries its count

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Decision owner:** Noell

## Context

A median of three films is a different fact from a median of forty. The smallest comparison group is 8 standalone films from 2000 onward.

## Decision

A bundle needs at least 3 usable films to be ranked; a group needs at least 5 for a conclusion to be stated (S14). Every figure is printed with its count.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A: No minimum, counts shown only

- **How it would work:** every group reported; the reader judges.
- **Why it is attractive:** nothing is hidden.
- **Why rejected:** a bundle of 2 films could top the ranking on one hit.

## Consequences

- **What this buys:** measured: 22 of 32 bundles rankable over all years; 12 rankable in both windows; both comparison groups clear 5 in both windows. The decision-impact table shows the leader unchanged with no minimum, 3 or 5.
- **What this costs:** The Lord of the Rings, the deal-note candidate, has exactly the minimum of 3 films.
- **What it costs the client if I am wrong:** a strong bundle with 2 films on the list is never shown.
- **What would make me revisit this:** a larger list, where 5 could become the bundle minimum too.

## What this survives

Both thresholds are settings.
