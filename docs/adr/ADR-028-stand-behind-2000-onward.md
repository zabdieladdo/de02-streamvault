# ADR-028: The shortlist and deal note stand behind the 2000-onward window; all-years figures are the caveat

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Decision owner:** Noell

## Context

All three shortlists differ between windows. Finance distrusts pre-2000 box office. The franchise-versus-standalone verdict is the same in both windows.

## Decision

The memo leads with the 2000-onward shortlist. The deal-note candidate is the bundle that is rankable in both windows and whose worse rank of the two is best, ties broken by the stronger weakest film (the higher walk-away ratio); on this snapshot The Lord of the Rings (2nd all years, 1st from 2000). The all-years picks are named in one line as strong on pre-2000 figures only.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A: Stand behind all years

- **How it would work:** lead with Star Wars, Lord of the Rings, Indiana Jones and the five classic standalones.
- **Why it is attractive:** uses all the data; the classics are real candidates.
- **Why rejected:** the lead picks rest on 1966-1994 figures Finance distrusts. Measured: Star Wars falls from 2.49× the median to 1.31× when its post-2000 films are compared within the 2000-onward window, and it has only 2 films from 2000, so it cannot be ranked there.

### Alternative B: Lead with only what appears in both windows

- **How it would work:** The Lord of the Rings and Spirited Away first, then the rest labelled by window.
- **Why it is attractive:** only the most robust picks appear unqualified.
- **Why rejected:** two short labelled lists are harder to read on one page.

## Consequences

- **What this buys:** every lead pick is defensible in the room. From 2000 onward all three franchise picks clear the list median (2.4×, 1.7×, 1.4×), weakest films at least 1.2×.
- **What this costs:** famous classics move to the caveat line; the 2000-onward standalone list is thin (5 of 8 films; 2 of 5 clear the median).
- **What it costs the client if I am wrong:** if pre-2000 figures are sound, Kwame passes over Star Wars and Indiana Jones, which led over all years.
- **What would make me revisit this:** Finance accepting an inflation-adjusted view of pre-2000 figures.

## What this survives

The rule is applied by the code each run; Adjoa's next list may produce a different candidate.
